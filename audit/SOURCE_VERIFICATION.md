## Cross-paper audit

The papers use different materials, apparatuses and operating modes. Therefore:

- no pooled regression across P1/P2/P3 is performed;
- no claim is made that P2 yield stress values belong to the P1 fluids;
- no claim is made that P3 response times directly predict P1 pinch-mode response;
- cross-paper conclusions are framed as mechanistic engineering synthesis.
